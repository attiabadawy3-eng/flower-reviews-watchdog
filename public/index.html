FLOWER Google Reviews
Google Business Profile review management for FLOWER Serviced Apartments
Internal operations service

Review management and analytics

FLOWER Google Reviews is an internal operations application used by authorized FLOWER Serviced Apartments staff. It connects approved Google Business Profile accounts to read guest reviews, prepare or publish responses, and produce operational review analytics for managed branches.

Why Google access is requested

The application requests Google Business Profile access only so authorized staff can manage the business profiles they control, including reading reviews and posting replies. Google account data is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Access

The operational dashboard is restricted to authorized FLOWER staff. This public page explains the application's purpose and data practices.
