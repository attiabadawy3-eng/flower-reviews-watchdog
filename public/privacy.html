FLOWER Google Reviews

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how FLOWER Google Reviews accesses and uses Google user data when an authorized FLOWER Serviced Apartments operator connects a Google Business Profile account.

Data we access

  • Basic Google account identity information used to identify an authorized connection.
  • OAuth access and refresh credentials required to maintain the authorized Google connection.
  • Google Business Profile account and location information.
  • Business Profile reviews, review replies, and related timestamps.
  • Business Profile performance and review analytics used for internal operational reporting.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the requested Business Profile management functions: discovering managed locations, reading reviews, preparing or posting replies, and producing review and visibility analytics for FLOWER operations.

Storage and security

Application data is stored using the service infrastructure used to operate FLOWER Google Reviews. OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted form. Access to the operational dashboard is restricted to authorized staff.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data and do not use it for advertising. Data is processed only by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service and as required to provide the requested functionality.

Google API Services User Data Policy

FLOWER Google Reviews' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

An authorized Google account owner can revoke the application's Google access from their Google Account permissions. Once access is revoked, the application can no longer retrieve new Google data for that connection unless authorization is granted again.

Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling can be sent to flowerhotell111@gmail.com.
